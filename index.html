About Me§

Welcome to my personal website! My name is Agnieszka Ziora. I’m a 25-year-old Polish researcher specialising in computational linguistic analysis, sentiment, values analysis and citizen science. I am located in Poland, currently doing a PhD in Doctoral School of University of Silesia.

The main aim of my PhD dissertation is the analysis of sentiment and values in citizen science online communication discourse within the framework of appraisal theory and with the use of corpus linguistics. For that purpose together with Dominik Gęgotek we are compiling a sizable citizen science corpus and a creating a corpus tool for its analysis. If you are interested in updates concerning their development, check the recent posts.

I am also a member of research club called Neolinguists and a member of the DIGESPEDI research team.
