My CV

Welcome to my personal website! My name is Agnieszka Gęgotek (de domo Ziora). I’m a 25-year-old Polish researcher specialising in computational linguistic analysis, sentiment, values analysis and citizen science. I am located in Poland, currently doing a PhD in Doctoral School of University of Silesia.I am also a member of research club called Neolinguists and a member of the DIGESPEDI research team.
